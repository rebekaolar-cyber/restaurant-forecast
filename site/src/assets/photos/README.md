Photos on the public pages.

- `dishes/` — one photo per dish, named after the dish (`hommus.jpg`). The file
  name goes in the dish's `photo` field in `src/content/menu.ts`. A dish without
  a photo shows a marked "Foto volgt" placeholder.
- `home/` — the four photos on the homepage that open the menu.

Use JPG, as large as you have it, in portrait (3:4). The build makes the small
AVIF and WebP versions. Only use photos the restaurant owns or has permission to
use, and none in which guests or staff can be recognised. The photos of the
entrance door and the interior in Rebeka's vault are not to be published.
